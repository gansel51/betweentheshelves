These are **reference search links and art briefs**, not copies of the images displayed in the original concept conversation and not licensed production assets. Use them to assemble an approved moodboard and commission/create original art. Check image licensing and attribution before reuse.

- [Magical library with stained glass and golden lighting](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=magical+storybook+library+interior+golden+lighting+painted+game+background)

**Brief:** A warmly lit library front desk in foreground, inviting rows of books receding into painterly depth, brass details, soft amber window light, forest green and walnut palette. Magical but grounded. Avoid generic 3D render, chibi characters, block figures, sterile UI.

## Art review checklist
- Does this feel like the *same* magical library world?
- Is the scene readable on a 6-inch phone?
- Can the asset be layered/animated economically?
- Are materials tactile, not generic flat gradients?
- Does the art preserve enough contrast for accessible UI?
- Is the image original, licensed, or otherwise cleared for intended use?
